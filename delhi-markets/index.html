Sadar Bazar & Rui Mandi

Sadar Bazar and its specialized alley Rui Mandi represent Asia's largest wholesale hub for imitation jewellery. However, navigating these crowded lanes comes with high MOQ barriers, lack of mix-and-match freedom, and unverified brass alloys marketed as stainless steel.

Metro Directions:
Take Yellow/Red line to Tis Hazari or Chandni Chowk, or skip the commute entirely by taking the Blue Line straight to Tagore Garden Metro Station (3 mins to Arora Group showroom).

Karol Bagh (Bank Street & Ajmal Khan Road)

Karol Bagh is celebrated for high-end gold, diamond, and silver showrooms along Bank Street, alongside busy artificial jewellery distributors around Ajmal Khan Road and Gaffar Market.

Metro Directions:
Board the Blue Line at Karol Bagh Metro Station and arrive at Tagore Garden Metro Station in just 15 minutes.

Chandni Chowk (Dariba Kalan & Kinari Bazar)

Dariba Kalan in Chandni Chowk has been Delhi's legendary silver and bridal jewellery quarter since the Mughal era. While unrivaled for traditional bridal and real silver, modern Western and Korean anti-tarnish pieces are scarce and overpriced.

Metro Directions:
Chandni Chowk Metro Station (Yellow Line) or direct drive via Ring Road to Tagore Garden.

Pitampura (Netaji Subhash Place & Rani Bagh)

Netaji Subhash Place (NSP) and Rani Bagh form North-West Delhi's commercial and fashion accessories axis. While Rani Bagh features traditional imitation jewellery stores, NSP houses dozens of modern D2C brand offices.
